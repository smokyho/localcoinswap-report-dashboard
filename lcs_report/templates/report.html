i Profit is measured in each trade's own quote currency — the leg it settled in, so buying BTC with IDR is quoted in IDR and anything settled in USD is quoted in USD. It is what that crypto was worth at that day's rate, minus what you paid. Each trade converts its USD side with the rate for its own date and quote currency, never a single global rate. Figures in different currencies are shown separately and never added.
Why this differs from a current-rate report: the at trade time column divides by the {{ base_currency }}/{{ display_currency }} rate published on the trade's own date, snapping back to the prior business day over weekends.
